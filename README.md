# French learning

A structured French grammar course for a complete beginner, with Markdown lessons and exercises, adaptive review, and vocabulary prepared for later Anki CSV exports.

## Start here
1. Study [A1 Lesson 1 — Articles and gender](grammar/a1/01_articles_and_gender.md).
2. Complete [Lesson 1 exercises](grammar/a1/01_articles_and_gender_exercises.md) and send your numbered answers in chat.
3. Review feedback and continue to the next lesson based on your progress.

No initial assessment is required.

## Repository guide
- [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) — consistent instructions for AI tutors
- [curriculum.md](curriculum.md) — proposed A1–B2 grammar sequence
- [progress.md](progress.md) — assessed performance and review targets
- [grammar/](grammar/) — lessons and exercises in separate Markdown files
- [practice/](practice/) — targeted and cumulative Markdown exercises
- [vocabulary/](vocabulary/) — canonical vocabulary table, ready for future Anki CSV generation

Grammar is explained directly in English and French, without comparisons to Spanish. Answers are reviewed after exercises are submitted, and progress is updated only after actual assessment.
