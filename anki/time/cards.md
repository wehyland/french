# Assimil French — Lesson 004: L’heure

Source: learner-supplied dialogue and three glossary screenshots, 2026-11-20. These are flashcard references with answers, separate from graded grammar exercises.

Say the French for Speaking and Vocabulary; give the English meaning for Comprehension; supply one missing word for Patterns. Equivalent grammatical responses are acceptable. Learn the phrases as chunks. Study Speaking answers and notes before Patterns; new grammar is explained there.

| # | Type | Front | French answer | Meaning | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Speaking | Ask politely: “Excuse me, do you have the time?” | Pardon, est-ce que vous avez l’heure ? | Excuse me, do you have the time? | Learn this polite question as a chunk. The s in vous links to avez with a z sound; the h in heure is silent. Accept equivalent polite questions; this is the lesson’s model. |
| 2 | Speaking | Say: “I don’t have a watch.” | Je n’ai pas de montre. | I don’t have a watch. | Ne…pas makes the statement negative; ne becomes n’ before ai. In this negative construction, une montre becomes pas de montre. |
| 3 | Speaking | Say the opening phrase: “I think that…” | Je pense que… | I think that… | Penser que + statement. Que becomes qu’ before il. Learn the supplied forms as chunks; no conjugation table is required. Only the opening phrase is required. |
| 4 | Speaking | Say: “It’s ten past noon.” | Il est midi dix. | It’s ten past noon. | Midi dix = 12:10 at midday. Il is an impersonal subject here. At the end of midi dix, the x in dix sounds like s. |
| 5 | Speaking | Say: “I have an appointment at noon.” | J’ai rendez-vous à midi. | I have an appointment at noon. | Avoir rendez-vous = to have an appointment; à midi = at noon. This expression has no article before rendez-vous. |
| 6 | Speaking | Say: “I have an appointment with my wife.” | J’ai rendez-vous avec ma femme. | I have an appointment with my wife. | Avoir rendez-vous avec + person. Ma femme means my wife here; learn the supplied possessive as part of the phrase. Femme sounds like fam. |
| 7 | Speaking | Say: “She is always ten minutes late.” | Elle a toujours dix minutes de retard. | She is always ten minutes late. | Avoir + duration + de retard = to be that much late. Retard is a masculine noun, delay/lateness. Before minutes, dix is pronounced di, without a final s. |
| 8 | Comprehension | Je n’ai pas de montre mais je pense qu’il est midi dix. | Je n’ai pas de montre mais je pense qu’il est midi dix. | I don’t have a watch, but I think it’s ten past noon. | Ne…pas makes the statement negative; ne becomes n’ before ai. In this negative construction, une montre becomes pas de montre. Mais = but; je pense qu’il est… = I think it is… Give the meaning; do not reproduce the sentence. |
| 9 | Comprehension | Mais comment le savez-vous ? | Mais comment le savez-vous ? | But how do you know? | Recognition chunk. Savez comes from savoir, to know; le refers to the information just mentioned, not the article the. Inversion is not a production requirement. Equivalent English translations are fine. |
| 10 | Comprehension | Parce que j’ai rendez-vous avec ma femme à midi. | Parce que j’ai rendez-vous avec ma femme à midi. | Because I have an appointment with my wife at noon. | Avoir rendez-vous avec + person. Ma femme means my wife here; learn the supplied possessive as part of the phrase. Femme sounds like fam. Parce que = because. Give the meaning; you are not being tested on constructing this longer sentence. |
| 11 | Vocabulary | Say: “an hour”. Include the indefinite article. | une heure | an hour | Feminine noun; definite form l’heure. Learn the gender with une; the h is silent. |
| 12 | Vocabulary | Say: “a watch”. Include the indefinite article. | une montre | a watch | Feminine noun. The example means I do not have a watch; negative pas de is explained on the phrase card. |
| 13 | Vocabulary | Say: “a woman / a wife”. Include the indefinite article. | une femme | a woman; a wife | Feminine noun. Ma femme means my wife in the dialogue. Femme sounds like fam. |
| 14 | Vocabulary | Say: “an appointment / a meeting”. Include the indefinite article. | un rendez-vous | an appointment; a meeting | Masculine noun. In avoir rendez-vous, omit the article. The final s in rendez-vous is silent. |
| 15 | Vocabulary | Say: “a minute”. Include the indefinite article. | une minute | a minute | Feminine noun. In dix minutes, the x in dix is silent. |
| 16 | Vocabulary | Say: “noon”. | midi | noon | Masculine noun, normally without an article in il est midi and à midi. |
| 17 | Vocabulary | Say: “because”. | parce que | because | Gives a reason. Que becomes qu’ before a vowel, as in parce qu’il…; before j’ai it remains parce que. |
| 18 | Vocabulary | Say: “always”. | toujours | always | Can also mean still in other contexts. The final s is silent. |
| 19 | Patterns | Complete with one word: Je n’ai pas ___ montre.<br>Meaning: I don’t have a watch. | Je n’ai pas de montre. | I don’t have a watch. | Ne…pas makes the statement negative; ne becomes n’ before ai. In this negative construction, une montre becomes pas de montre. Missing word: de. Read the short rule on the back of the no-watch phrase card before trying this pattern. |
| 20 | Patterns | Complete with one word: J’ai rendez-vous ___ midi.<br>Meaning: I have an appointment at noon. | J’ai rendez-vous à midi. | I have an appointment at noon. | Avoir rendez-vous = to have an appointment; à midi = at noon. This expression has no article before rendez-vous. Missing word: à. Learn à midi as a chunk. |
| 21 | Patterns | Complete with one word: Elle a dix minutes de ___.<br>Meaning: She is ten minutes late. | Elle a dix minutes de retard. | She is ten minutes late. | Avoir + duration + de retard = to be that much late. Retard is a masculine noun, delay/lateness. Before minutes, dix is pronounced di, without a final s. Missing word: retard. Learn avoir dix minutes de retard as a chunk. |
