# A1 Lesson 1 — Nouns, articles, and grammatical gender

**Goal:** Identify masculine and feminine nouns and choose the correct singular or plural article. By the end, you should be able to form simple noun phrases such as *a book*, *the house*, and *some tables*.

## 1. French nouns have grammatical gender

Every French noun is grammatically **masculine** or **feminine**. The gender is part of the word; it doesn't necessarily describe the object.

| Masculine | Feminine |
| --- | --- |
| **un livre** — a book | **une table** — a table |
| **un café** — a coffee | **une maison** — a house |
| **un ami** — a male friend | **une amie** — a female friend |

**Study habit:** Learn a noun together with its article, for example **une maison**, rather than memorizing only *maison*. Noun endings sometimes suggest gender but have exceptions.

## 2. Indefinite articles: a, an, some

Use an **indefinite article** when referring to a person or thing without identifying a particular one.

| | Masculine | Feminine |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Singular | **un** livre | **une** table |
| Plural | **des** livres | **des** tables |

In the plural, **des** is used for both genders. The singular articles **un** and **une** do **not** shorten before vowels: **un ami**, **une amie**.

## 3. Definite articles: the

Use a **definite article** when identifying a particular noun, or in some contexts when speaking about things generally.

| | Masculine | Feminine |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Singular | **le** livre | **la** table |
| Plural | **les** livres | **les** tables |

Before a vowel sound or **h muet** (silent, non-aspirated h), **le** and **la** become **l’**:
- **l'ami** — the male friend
- **l'amie** — the female friend
- **l'école** — the school

**Important:** The form **l’** hides the noun's gender. **Une école** is feminine, while **un ami** is masculine. Some French words beginning with *h* (called *h aspiré*) do not allow this shortening, e.g. **le héros**. Learn these as exceptions later.

## 4. Singular and plural nouns

Most French nouns add a written **-s** in the plural. This final **-s** is usually silent.

- **le livre → les livres**
- **une maison → des maisons**

Some nouns form plurals differently; we will deal with those later. For now, use the regular **-s** pattern.

## 5. Two uses worth noticing

- **Un livre** = a book; **le livre** = the book.
- French can also use the definite article for general categories: **J'aime le café** = I like coffee. (You don't need to know the verb *aimer* yet.)

## 6. Common mistakes

- Gender: **une maison**, not *un maison*.
- Number: **les livres**, not *le livres*.
- Elision: **l'école**, not *la école*.
- Before a vowel, indefinite articles remain **un** and **une**: **une amie**.
